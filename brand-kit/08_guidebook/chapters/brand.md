## Purpose · উদ্দেশ্য

GRU953 exists to make genuinely simple, honest technology that anyone can use — whatever
their money, language, or ability. Tools should not demand that people become experts, buy
their way in, or leave their language at the door.

GRU953-এর জন্ম সত্যিকারের সহজ, সৎ প্রযুক্তি বানানোর জন্য — যা যে কেউ ব্যবহার করতে পারেন; টাকা, ভাষা
বা সামর্থ্য যা-ই হোক। কোনো টুল যেন মানুষকে বিশেষজ্ঞ হতে, টাকা দিয়ে ঢুকতে, বা নিজের ভাষা দরজার
বাইরে রেখে আসতে বাধ্য না করে।

## The tagline

> ## Simple technology. For everyone.
> ### সহজ প্রযুক্তি। সবার জন্য।

Four words in each language. It is a promise, not a slogan, which is why it is
written as two flat statements and not as wordplay. Use it complete. Do not shorten it to
“Simple technology”, do not extend it, and do not translate it into a third language
without checking that the second sentence still lands as a promise rather than a boast.

## The three pillars · তিনটি স্তম্ভ

### 1. Simple by design · গোড়া থেকেই সহজ

Build only what the task needs. Every feature, word and pixel must earn its place; when in
doubt, leave it out.

কাজে যতটুকু লাগে, ততটুকুই বানান। প্রতিটি ফিচার, শব্দ ও পিক্সেলকে নিজের জায়গা অর্জন করতে হবে;
সন্দেহ হলে বাদ দিন।

### 2. For everyone · সবার জন্য

Bilingual by default, accessible by default, offline-friendly by default, and free to use.

স্বভাবতই দ্বিভাষিক, স্বভাবতই প্রবেশগম্য, ইন্টারনেট ছাড়াও সচল, আর ব্যবহারের জন্য বিনামূল্যে।

### 3. Honest craft · সৎ কারিগরি

No hype, no dark patterns, no guesses presented as facts. Claims are computed, limits are
stated, and quality is checked before it ships.

কোনো অতিকথন নয়, কোনো ছলচাতুরী নয়, অনুমানকে সত্য বলে চালানো নয়। দাবি হিসাব করে বলা হয়,
সীমাবদ্ধতা স্পষ্ট করা হয়, মান যাচাই করে তবেই ছাড়া হয়।

## The four values · চারটি মূল্যবোধ

1. **Simplicity first.** · আগে সরলতা।
2. **Care for every user.** · প্রতিটি ব্যবহারকারীর যত্ন।
3. **Honesty.** · সততা।
4. **Craft.** · কারিগরি নিষ্ঠা।

If a decision serves one value by betraying another, it is the wrong decision — find the

এমন পথ খুঁজুন।

## What GRU953 is

A **solo software studio**. One developer, in Bangladesh, building tools and shipping them
in the open. It is built to grow into a small product house — where individual apps carry
their own names under GRU953 — but it is not one today, and the brand never pretends
otherwise.

## Who it is for, in order

The order matters, because a brand that tries to please four audiences equally pleases none.

| Priority | Audience | What they are looking for | How the design answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Developers and the open-source community** | Signals of technical taste: restraint, precision, things that actually work | Computed contrast rather than claimed. A mark with a size floor that is checked by a script, not asserted. Tokens you can paste into code. No decoration without a reason. |
| 2 | **Employers and recruiters** | Evidence of range and of finishing things | A complete, documented system. Governance documents. Honest statements of limits. |
| 3 | **Paying clients** | Reassurance that the job will be done | Clear licence terms. Templates for invoices and proposals. A tone that does not oversell. |
| 4 | **End users of the apps** | Warmth, plain language, their own language | Bilingual by default. Plain words. Error messages that help rather than blame. |

Designing for developers first is not a snub to the others. A developer brand that impresses
developers reads as competent to a recruiter, trustworthy to a client, and careful to a user.
The reverse is not true.

## Personality

**Precise and quietly confident, and warm.** Those two together are the whole character, and
the combination is where the distinctiveness lives — most engineering brands pick precision
and end up cold, while most “friendly” brands pick warmth and end up imprecise.

In practice:

- Exact about numbers, generous about people.
- Calm. Never shouts, never uses an exclamation mark to manufacture enthusiasm.
- Says “I don’t know” out loud.
- Nothing decorative that has not earned its place.

## What GRU953 is not

Stating this is more useful than another adjective:

- Not a startup. No growth language, no “we’re on a mission to disrupt”.
- Not an agency or a team. There is no “we” pretending to be several people.
- Not enterprise software. No jargon used as a credential.
- Not a personal blog brand. The work is the subject, not the person’s opinions.
- Not playful for its own sake. Warmth comes from clarity and care, not from jokes.

## The positioning statement

For **developers, employers and clients** who want to see whether one person can build
software properly, **GRU953** is a **solo software studio** that makes **genuinely simple,
accessible, bilingual tools and gives the whole system away**. Unlike a portfolio that shows
finished screenshots, GRU953 shows the working — the computed contrast, the stated limits,
the licence that actually permits what it promises.

**Showing the working is the brand.** In practice that means one habit: whenever GRU953
claims something measurable, publish the measurement beside it. A contrast ratio, a file
size, a load time, a limit. That is the whole differentiator, and it is a thing to do rather
than a thing to say.
