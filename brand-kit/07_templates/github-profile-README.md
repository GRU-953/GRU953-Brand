## English

Hello, I'm Aninda. I write software on my own, under the name GRU953 — a one-person
software studio — from Bangladesh.

The work is small tools built for people the industry tends to skip: someone on a
five-year-old Android, on a patchy connection, reading Bangla. So the apps here open fast,
work offline, and speak both languages from the first screen. The design system I build
them with is open under Apache-2.0, so take any of it.

### What I build

- Small applications that do one thing, in Bangla and English, from the first screen.
- Offline first, because the connection is not always there.
- Built for old and cheap phones, not just for the phone I own.
- No account to create, no advert, no data leaving the device unless I say so plainly.
- The design system behind all of it, published for anyone to use.

### The three pillars

- **Simple by design** — given two ways to do something, I take the simpler one. Fewer
  screens, not less care.
- **For everyone** — money, language and ability should not decide who gets to use
  software.
- **Honest craft** — limits are named out loud, and a claim arrives with its number or it
  does not get made.

দ্রুত চালু হয়, ইন্টারনেট ছাড়াও চলে, আর প্রথম পর্দা থেকেই দুই ভাষায় কথা বলে। যে ডিজাইন
